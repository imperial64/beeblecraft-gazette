Gazette Builder

Lay out a newspaper in the Beeblecraft Gazette's pixel style and export each page as a 1200 by 1600 PNG. Everything happens in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Making an issue

  1. Drag a block from the toolbox on the right onto the page. While you drag, the page shows how it will look with the block dropped there, the new block faded and outlined. A click on a tile adds the block at the end of the selected block's column instead, which also works on a phone.
  2. Click any text on the page and type.
  3. Drop screenshots from your files onto a photo to fill it, or anywhere else on the page to add them as new photos. An empty photo also has an Add photo button.
  4. Click a block to edit it in the inspector on the left. Click empty paper to edit the page itself.
  5. The + next to the page tabs adds a blank page or one from a preset. Load sample opens issue 1 of the Beeblecraft Gazette to look at.

Bands and columns

A page is a stack of bands, each with its own columns. While you drag a block, bars show between the bands and strips at the edges and between the columns. Drop on a bar for a new band holding just that block, at the left or right edge of a band or between two columns for a new column; a "+" marks the one you are on. A column that loses its last block goes away. Three columns is the format the paper was designed for; more are allowed and get a warning.

A block can run across several columns. Drag the small tab on the right edge of a block sideways over the columns it should cover, or set Span columns in the inspector. A block keeps its span whatever you put above or beside it, so the column to its left runs on beside it.

Moving blocks

Point at a block and a coloured strip with its name shows above it. Drag the strip to move the block anywhere: into a column, to a new column, or onto a bar for a band of its own. The page shows how it will look before you let go, with the block faded where it will land. A drag on the block itself selects text or moves a photo's crop. Escape cancels a drag. Alt and the arrow keys move a selected block up and down its column or to the next column. Delete removes it, and Ctrl+Z undoes.

Masthead and footer

The masthead and the footer are blocks like any other. The Masthead and Footer tiles add them, they can be dragged anywhere on the page as a band of their own, and deleting them leaves a bare page. A footer at the end of the page sits at the bottom edge. The wordmark, tagline and kickers are shared by every masthead in the issue.

Text

Click to type. Enter starts a new paragraph, or ends a one-line text such as a headline. The bar above the text sets the font, size and colour of that whole text, and can upload a font file (.ttf, .otf, .woff, .woff2). A colour can be one of the theme's or any hex code; Save colour keeps it in the issue for later.

Photos

Drop an image onto a photo, or use Add photo or Upload photo. Select the photo, then turn the mouse wheel over it to zoom and drag it to move the crop. The strip along its bottom edge sets a fixed height; Flexible in the inspector lets it stretch with the column again. Photos and the map stretch to fill their column down to the end of the band. A photo across several columns stretches only when nothing follows it in any of them.

Theme

Theme in the top bar edits the look of the whole issue: every colour as a hex code, the default font, size and colour of each kind of text, the lantern and chain sprites (your own PNG, up to 64 by 64), uploaded fonts and the paper texture. Export theme saves it as a file to use in another issue; Import theme loads one.

Issues and warnings

The triangle above the page is red when something will export wrong (a column runs over, a photo is missing, a character the font does not have) and yellow when there are only warnings (a gap at the bottom of a column, an empty column, a missing credit). Hover it for the count and click it for the list; click a line to go to that block. A small dot on a block marks it too.

Save versus export

Save writes the issue file (.json) that you reopen and share with a co-editor. Export PNG writes the pictures you post. Export text writes the words only.

The browser also keeps a draft of the issue on its own, so closing the tab loses nothing. Drafts belong to this browser and this address only (the website and a downloaded copy of the file keep separate drafts). In Chrome and Edge, Save and Ctrl+S write back to the file you opened; other browsers download a new copy each time. Export all pages saves one file per page; Chrome may ask once to allow several downloads.
